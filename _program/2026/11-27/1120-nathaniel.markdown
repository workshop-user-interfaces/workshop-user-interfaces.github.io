3D-printed, patient-customized hand orthoses offer superior comfort and patient satisfaction compared to conventional casts. However, their clinical adoption is still hindered by manual and time-consuming design processes. This study presents an automated parametric design pipeline to generate print-ready 3D hand-wrist orthosis models from patient 3D scan data. The proposed method ntegrates multi-view MediaPipe-based computer vision with Direct Linear Transformation (DLT) to detect anatomical hand landmarks. Subsequently, the system automatically generates the orthosis geometry, incorporating an ulnar-side opening, velcro strap slots, and a Voronoi ventilation pattern. Experimental results demonstrate that the entire pipeline generates a print-ready 3D model in under one minute.
