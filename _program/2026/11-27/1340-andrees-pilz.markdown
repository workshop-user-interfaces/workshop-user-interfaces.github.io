speakers: "Josina Andrees, Clara Pilz"

This study builds on last semester’s research and compares two improved interchange station designs in schematic transit maps. Participants will rate both designs before and after completing navigation tasks to see how using the maps changes their preferences. The study also examines how visual appeal relates to wayfinding performance.
