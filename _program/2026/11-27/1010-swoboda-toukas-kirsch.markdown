title: "Multisensory Design of Workspaces to Promote Focused Mental States"

Students and knowledge workers often lack access to work environments that support sustained concentration during extended work sessions. This leads to our research question: How can multisensory design in immersive work environments help students and knowledge workers improve focus, sustain attention, and reach a state of flow? To address this question, we conduct a literature review in which each team member examines one design dimension of a work booth, such as sound, visual design, lighting, scent, tactile interaction, or the structure of the booth itself. For each dimension, we analyze its psychological effects on users. Together, these perspectives converge into an initial concept for a work booth supporting 2–3 hour work sessions, which will be prototypically implemented and evaluated in future work.
