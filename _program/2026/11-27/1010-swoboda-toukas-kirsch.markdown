We are investigating how a workspace can be enhanced through multisensory design to help users reach and maintain focused mental states. Our goal is to design a prototype work booth that supports focused work sessions of 2–3 hours for students or knowledge workers. In the future, such a booth could be placed in libraries or similar facilities. Through a literature review, each team member explores one area of the booth, such as sound, visual design, lighting, scent, tactile interaction, or stress measurement through vital signs. Together, these areas form a coherent concept and address our overarching research question: How can immersive environments, technologies, and media help students and knowledge workers improve focus, sustain attention, and reach a state of flow?
