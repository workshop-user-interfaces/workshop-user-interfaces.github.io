What is the best way to describe to an AI how something should feel? This research project investigates how people communicate with artificial intelligence to generate vibrotactile patterns. The focus is not on the technical implementation, but on the interface between human and machine. The research is investigating which inputs – such as text, images, video or multimodal data – lead to a desired vibrotactile pattern. To verify this, specific application scenarios are defined and the AI results are evaluated directly against a fixed benchmark using a prototype.
