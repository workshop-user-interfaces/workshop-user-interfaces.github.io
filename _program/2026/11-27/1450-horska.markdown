title: "Haptic Communicator for Users with Specific Needs in a Healthcare Context"

Development of the concept and physical design of a haptic communicator enabling touch transfer between a patient in a clinical environment and their relatives in a home setting. The product functions as a supplementary patient care tool situated within the context of palliative care, which sustaining interpersonal contact in situations where physical interaction is limited.
