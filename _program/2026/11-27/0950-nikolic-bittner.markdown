Guide dogs are an important mobility aid for blind and visually impaired people, yet their availability is limited and waiting times are long. RobDog is a quadruped guide robot developed at HTW Dresden to support independent mobility in everyday environments. This talk focuses on human-robot interaction: how the robot and its user communicate during guidance and which requirements this places on interface design.
