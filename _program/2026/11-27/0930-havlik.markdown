This submission describes how people with visual impairments acquire spatial knowledge in the form of a cognitive map and how this process can be supported in virtual environments. It presents current approaches used in multimodal VR systems for people with visual impairments and shows how they are applied in a system specialized for cognitive map acquisition developed by the author.
