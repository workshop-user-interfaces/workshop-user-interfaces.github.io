The goal of this project is to design a system that assists older adults in their everyday life and helps them live independently at home for longer. The design is divided into three main pillars: system-initiated reminders, user-initiated conversation such as troubleshooting and stress mitigation, and assistance with controlling household appliances using voice interaction and simplified interfaces. The overarching philosophy of the design process is to offer a helping hand when needed, without forcing the user’s hand or making decisions on their behalf.
