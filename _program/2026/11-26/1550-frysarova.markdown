This work-in-progress project explores how features such as goals, streaks, and automated feedback in fitness apps may create pressure for women with lived experience of eating disorders. Drawing on consultations with professionals, I examine how a digital tool could support self-reflection and give users space to make sense of their experiences in their own words.
