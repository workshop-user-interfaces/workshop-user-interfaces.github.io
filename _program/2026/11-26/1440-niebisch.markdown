Multisensory media environments complement audiovisual content with further sensory stimuli such as airflow. This talk presents a software-controlled wind actuation system that plays back time-based wind scenes on multiple fans in sync with an existing media player, using a hardware abstraction layer to separate playback from the fans. A prototype including a scene editor was implemented and functionally tested on a tabletop setup with three screens and three miniature fans; it provides a basis for further development towards the multisensory interaction space at HTW Dresden.
