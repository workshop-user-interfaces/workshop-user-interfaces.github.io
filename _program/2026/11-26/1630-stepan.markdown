VENT-CONNECT is a platform for remote monitoring of mechanically ventilated ICU patients. We interviewed five physicians and let each of them redesign a simplified interactive mockup of the current interface through an AI coding agent. We present their assessment of the existing components, the prototypes they created, and what this suggests about generative AI in clinical participatory design.
